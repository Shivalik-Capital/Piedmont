# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Finance professionals, retail investors, economists, researchers, and anyone focused on the Indian market.

## Product Purpose
A dense, Bloomberg-like macroeconomic terminal tracking RBI rates, GDP, CPI, and Nifty metrics.

## Positioning
The most data-dense, at-a-glance macroeconomic dashboard tailored specifically for the Indian economy.

## Brand Personality
Professional, dense, and authoritative (like a modern Bloomberg Terminal).

## Anti-references
It should NOT look like a generic, fluffy SaaS dashboard with too much whitespace and huge rounded corners.

## Design Principles
- Data over decoration: Maximize information density; every pixel must serve a purpose.
- Expert confidence: Assume the user understands the data; avoid dumbing things down or over-explaining.
- Immediate clarity: The most critical macroeconomic indicators must be readable at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard accessibility with high contrast text and clearly readable data tables.
